General Assembly

Two committees. Global challenges in water access and biotechnology.

01 / GENERAL ASSEMBLY

FAO

Food and Agriculture Organization

Global Groundwater Depletion and Clean Water Access

Groundwater supports agriculture and communities, but overuse threatens water supplies. Delegates will explore how countries can protect groundwater, promote sustainable farming, and expand equitable access to clean water.

02 / GENERAL ASSEMBLY

DISEC

Disarmament and International Security Committee

The Benefits and Harms of Biotechnology

Biotechnology offers advances in health, agriculture, and science, while also raising concerns about misuse and international security. Delegates will consider how countries can encourage beneficial innovation while reducing biological risks and strengthening international cooperation.
