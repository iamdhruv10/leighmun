LEIGH HIGH SCHOOL / MODEL UN

Curiosity brings us together.

A student community exploring international affairs through research, debate, and diplomacy.

More than a debate.

Learn to see the world from another point of view.

In Model UN, students represent countries, research global challenges, and negotiate resolutions. Our club is a place to practice public speaking, develop thoughtful arguments, and collaborate with fellow delegates.

We meet every Thursday at lunch in Room 39.
